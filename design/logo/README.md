# Munyati logo — chosen direction

**Decision:** B + A mix (chosen by the owner).

| Use | Asset |
|---|---|
| Primary logo (website, splash, marketing, App Store screenshots) | `concept-b-wordmark/` — calligraphic منيتي with diamond dots, gold wish-star and swash, plus "MUNYATI" |
| App icon, favicon, social avatar, notification badge | `concept-a-monogram/` — meem "م" drawn as a solitaire ring with the wish sparkle |

Both share the brand palette (burgundy `#8A0D3A`, gold `#DFC389`, cream `#F2E5D2`, ivory `#FAFAEC`) and the diamond/sparkle motif, so they read as one family.

Concept C (`concept-c-symbol/`) is kept for reference only.

**Before launch:** have a calligrapher refine the B wordmark (it is built on the Amiri typeface) and confirm the swash + star is not read as a crescent motif.
